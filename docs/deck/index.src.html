Bidwire
The fee is charged only on a verified sale.
CPA, not CPM. The fee a merchant bids is charged only when an agent buys a verified offer.
That fee is our take rate. No impressions. No clicks. No pay-to-rank.
Wedge: DTC merchants and agent shopping apps.
Long game: the trust and reputation layer for agent commerce.

Why now

18 months agoYC kickoff

No MCP. No ACP. No agent had a credit card or a login.

This year

Agents got wallets and protocols: MCP and ACP.

Nowparaphrased

Paul Graham: Amazon banning agents is the biggest opening since Amazon. People will want agents to buy things for them.

Search ads are a ~$200B+/yr business built on human attention. Agents don't look at ads, so that spend has to compete on verifiable offers.
